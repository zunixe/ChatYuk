> Panduan gaya + template siap pakai untuk promosi ChatYuk (WhatsApp Status,
> Instagram/TikTok caption, Telegram, X, broadcast in-app, push, store).

> ⚠️ **WAJIB BACA dulu** bagian "Aturan Framing" di bawah — frasa tertentu
> bikin ChatYuk ditolak/di-ban di Play & Uptodown (insiden nyata, lihat AGENTS.md).

## 1. Aturan Framing (WAJIB)

ChatYuk = **aplikasi chat, komunitas, dan berbagi momen**. Bukan aplikasi
kenalan/dating. Semua materi promosi harus konsisten dengan framing ini.

### ✅ AMAN — pakai ini
- "chat dengan teman, keluarga, dan komunitas"
- "chat with friends, family, and communities"
- "join topic-based chat rooms" / "gmabung room sesuai topik"
- "berbagi foto & momen" / "share photos and moments"
- "privat and aman" / "private and secure"
- "nyaman, bebas iklan" / "clean, ad-free"
- "status online, idle, dan offline"
- "moderasi & fitur lapor" / "built-in moderation and reporting"

### ❌ DILARANG — jangan pernah tulis
- `meet new people` / `make new friends` / `cari teman baru` / `kenalan`
- `nearby people` / `people finder` / `orang sekitar` / `location-based discovery`
- `filter by gender, age, city` / `filter gender` / `cari berdasarkan umur/kota`
- `send coins and gifts to your favorite people` / `top up coins` / `withdraw`
- `dating` / `match` / `flirt` / `profile browsing`

### Aturan panjang teks
- Short description store: **maks 70 karakter**.
- Full description: **minimal 100 kata**.
- Tagline sosial media: **1 baris, maks ~90 karakter** biar tidak terpotong.
